Watch the whole masked video once before you share it. Automatic masking can miss people in some frames (fast movement, poor light, people at the edge). The sound is removed, because voices identify people too. The CSV files hold the tracked points per frame (x and y from 0 to 1, relative to the frame), and settings.json records exactly how the video was masked, for your data documentation.

Built on Google MediaPipe (Apache 2.0) and the Masked-Piper method (Owoyele, Trujillo, de Melo & Pouw, 2022). For long recordings, batches or precision masking, see MaskAnyone. Dev mode, still in development: please report problems to babajide.owoyele@ru.nl.
